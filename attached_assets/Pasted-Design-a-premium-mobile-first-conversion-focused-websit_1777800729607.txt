2) Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Now the real part. This is what you should submit or build from.

Product Name

Smart Farming Equipment as a Service (FaaS) Platform

1. Product Overview

A mobile-first web platform that connects farmers with tractor and agricultural equipment owners through a structured booking system, supported by village-level operators and AI-based insights.

Goal:
Solve equipment accessibility and utilization problems in rural farming.

This is not just a website—it’s a service platform.

Current rural issues:

Delays → crop loss
No pricing transparency
